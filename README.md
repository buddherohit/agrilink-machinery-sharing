# 🚜 AgriLink

### Smart Agricultural Machinery Sharing Platform

> Making agricultural machinery more affordable, accessible, and efficiently utilized through trusted sharing.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

**Omni_AgriTech_20 — Shared Access to Agricultural Machinery**

Small and marginal farmers often cannot afford expensive agricultural machinery, while machinery owned by other farmers may remain unused for long periods.

This creates problems such as:

- High machinery purchase costs
- Limited access to equipment
- Inefficient machinery utilization
- Difficulty finding nearby available machinery
- Lack of transparency in rental arrangements
- Trust issues between farmers and machinery owners

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**AgriLink** is a location-aware digital platform that connects farmers who need agricultural machinery with nearby farmers or machinery owners who have equipment available for sharing or rental.

Farmers can discover, compare, book, and track agricultural machinery through a simple and trusted platform.
